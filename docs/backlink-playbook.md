## Tier 2 — Profile & platform links (~1 hour)

- **LinkedIn company page** — create one; link studysync.co.za. Also add the
  site to the founder's personal LinkedIn profile.
- **Crunchbase** (free profile) — https://www.crunchbase.com
- **YouTube channel** — even one demo video; channel "About" links are indexed.
- **TikTok / X (Twitter) bios** — if accounts exist or are planned.
- **GitHub organisation profile** — if any public repos exist.
- **Product Hunt** — launch StudySync as a product; strong tech-audience link.
- **AlternativeTo** — list StudySync as an alternative to SimpleStudy/PhotoMath.

## Tier 3 — Editorial & community links (ongoing, highest value)

1. **Education journalists / blogs**: pitch the two blog articles
   (AI in education; AI cure-or-virus) to SA education media — Parent24,
   BusinessTech education desk, Daily Maverick education, EWN education.
   A single editorial link outweighs 50 directory links.
2. **School partnerships**: any school using StudySync → ask for a link from
   the school website's "resources" page. School (.co.za school domains,
   sometimes .ac.za) links are highly trusted in this niche.
3. **University student portals / SRC resource pages**: past-paper links are a
   natural fit for "matric resources" pages.
4. **Teacher Facebook groups & forums**: share past-paper pages where allowed
   (nofollow but drives traffic + brand searches, which do influence ranking).
5. **HARO-style / journalist requests**: respond to #JournoRequest on X for
   education/AI comment as "StudySync founder".
6. **Scholarship or free-resource pages**: many SA sites maintain
   "free matric resources" lists — email them:
   - matric.co.za style resource aggregators
   - careersportal.co.za resources section
   - eduladder-type listing sites

## Tier 4 — Content-led link magnets (already partially shipped)

- `/past-papers/*` hub pages are natural link targets — when emailing anyone,
  link to the *specific* subject page, not the homepage.
- The blog articles are citation-worthy; each new evidence-based article is a
  potential source for teachers and journalists.
- Consider a downloadable "Matric study timetable PDF" — printable freebies
  attract organic links from teacher blogs.

## Consistency rules (NAP)

Use the **identical** name, address and phone number on every listing —
inconsistent NAP data dilutes local-SEO trust. The canonical set is the block
above. Always link to `https://studysync.co.za` (https, no www, no trailing
slash) to consolidate link equity.

## What NOT to do

- Do not buy backlink packages (Fiverr "1000 backlinks" etc.) — these trigger
  Google spam penalties and can de-rank the site.
- Do not spam links in comment sections.
- Do not use private blog networks (PBNs).
- Quality over quantity: 10 real SA education-related links beat 1,000 junk ones.
